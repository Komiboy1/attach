Phone Number Lookup

Paste a phone number with its country code to see where it is from and how it should be written.

Start with + and the country code. Spaces, dashes and brackets are fine.

    Behind the scenes

    What happens when you press Look up.

    1. Spaces, dashes and brackets are removed.
    2. The leading digits are matched against a table of country calling codes.
    3. The remaining digits are checked against that country’s usual length.

    Good to know: this runs in your browser using a small built-in table. It cannot tell who owns a number or which network it is on.
